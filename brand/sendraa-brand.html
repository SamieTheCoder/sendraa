Rules for the brand

These hold whichever logo and scheme you pick.

  • The app follows Apple's design. SF Pro, SF Symbols, system controls and system colours. The brand shows up as the tint, the app icon and the wordmark on the sign-in screen.
  • Tint is for what you tap. Buttons, links and the selected tab use the light tint in light mode and the dark tint in dark mode. Body text stays in system colours.
  • Brand colour is a fill. It fills the app icon, the launch screen and filled buttons, with the on-brand colour for the label. It is never used for text on white.
  • Two typefaces. League Gothic for the wordmark and marketing headlines only. SF everywhere inside the app.
  • Errors use system red with an icon. This matters most for Postbox red, where colour alone cannot tell an error from the brand.
  • Smallest mark is 16 px. Below that, use the brand colour tile alone. Keep clear space around the mark equal to its height.
