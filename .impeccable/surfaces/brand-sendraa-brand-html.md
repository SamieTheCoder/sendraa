## Scope

Brand explorations board for Sendraa (`brand/sendraa-brand.html`). Mode: operate. The identity serves the mobile mail app first. The board follows the user's `agreementoo-brand.html` format: switchable logo concepts and colour schemes, with live previews of the app, the invite email and the icons. The board is also the decision surface. The user picks a logo and a scheme in the page.

## Audience and job

A founder choosing Sendraa's identity before the app UI is built. They need to compare marks and palettes on real product screens: inbox, mailbox admin, onboarding, invite email, app icon.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Sendraa is the telegram office for a startup. Mail arrives as pasted strips on a yellow form. It refuses the category default of a white Gmail clone with a blue accent and an envelope icon.

OWN-WORLD: Telegraph yellow owns the shell (top bar, tab bar, primary fills). Ink and form-paper neutrals come from one fixed ramp. Stamp red is used only for failure states. League Gothic is the display voice; the system UI face carries body and controls. A tape strip with a swallowtail cut on one end and a send point on the other is the mark.

STORY: The founder sees one identity across the app, email and icon. They understand that receiving is free and that sending has a visible allowance. They pick a logo and scheme in the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar: the lockup on the left; scheme pills, a capitals toggle and the dark toggle on the right. Below, a two-column hero. Left: the headline "Professional email on your own domain." in League Gothic at display size, with one line of copy. Right: a yellow panel holding the lockup at large size on a pasted strip. Primary action: choosing a concept in the next band.

FORM: Telegram form, position 7 of 7 on my ordered list. seed:ee378c54479359dc

SIGNATURE MOVE: Unread mail is a pasted strip. The subject sits on a white strip raised off the form; opening it presses the strip flat.

RAISES:
- From exposure record: every neutral comes from one fixed nine-step ink-to-paper ramp; no ad-hoc greys.
- From centre-rail setting: rules are hairlines; red appears only where it means failure.
- From cassette j-card: sending capacity is drawn as a length of tape used against its limit (500/day Gmail, 3,000/month Cloudflare).
- From character catalog: every mailbox row carries fixed nameplate furniture: name, address, role.
- From transit diagram: one closed set of delivery states: Delivered, Queued, Bounced.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Alternates in the board

- Pick: Mailbox bank (enamel green and brass, letterbox-door mark). Risk: closer to familiar "trusted utility" palettes.
- Competitive: Graphite (one neutral scale, one signal-orange accent). Holds product clarity, weak on identity.
- Canon: the standard email app look (blue, envelope mark), offered straight.

## Open

- Wordmark casing (lowercase is shown; not a commitment).
- Platform domain is undecided, so no URL appears in the board.

## Scope change (user decision)

The user removed the app screens from the board because the app will follow Apple's design. The brand now lives in the tint colour, the app icon and the wordmark. The inbox signature move and the sending-allowance tape no longer appear on the board; they belong to the app build if the app ever departs from system styling. The user also asked for more palettes: the board now carries eleven, each with a light and a dark tint that pass WCAG AA on white, black and #1C1C1E.
